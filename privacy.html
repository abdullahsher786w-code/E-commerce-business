At Exclusive, we respect your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you shop with us, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect information in three ways:

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, and order details when you create an account, place an order, or contact support.
  • Payment information: handled by our payment partners. We do not store your full card number on our servers.
  • Information collected automatically: your device type, browser, IP address, and the pages you view, so we can keep the site working and improve it.

How we use your information

  • Process and deliver your orders, and handle returns and refunds.
  • Manage your account, wishlist, and cart.
  • Send order updates and, if you subscribe, offers such as our 10% first-order discount.
  • Answer your questions and provide customer support.
  • Detect fraud and keep the store secure.
  • Understand how people use the site so we can improve it.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Delivery and logistics partners, so your order reaches you.
  • Payment processors, to complete your purchase.
  • Service providers who help us run the website, such as hosting and email.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in, remember your cart and wishlist, and measure site traffic. You can turn cookies off in your browser settings, but parts of the store, like checkout, may stop working.

Allowed

Data security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and limit who can access your data. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a strong, unique password and do not share it with anyone.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong or out of date.
  • Delete your account and personal data, unless we must keep some records by law.
  • Stop sending you marketing emails. Use the unsubscribe link in any email, or write to us.

Children's privacy

Exclusive is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Policy changes

When we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we will also email account holders.
